Benefits of Honey

Rich in Antioxidants

Many of us enjoy honey and incorporate it into our daily diet, whether as a natural sugar substitute for coffee or simply as a sweet treat.

Natural Cough Medicine

Honey is a time-honored remedy for a sore throat. You can create your own remedy at home by mixing honey with herbal tea or warm water and lemon.

Lowers Blood Pressure

Blood pressure is an important risk factor for heart disease, and honey may help lower it.

Improves Cholesterol

Studies have shown that consuming honey lowers LDL cholesterol and may increase HDL cholesterol.

Improves Heart Health

Honey contains antioxidants which may support better heart health and help improve cholesterol levels.

Antibacterial Properties

Honey contains hydrogen peroxide which may account for some of its antibacterial properties.
